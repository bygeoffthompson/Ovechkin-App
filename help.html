Goal Search

Goals are identified by a unique number.

NHL regular season goals are indexed 1 to 929. All other goals are intertwined as floats.

  • 0.02 is World Championship Goal #2
  • 21 is NHL Goal #21
  • 36.04 is Olympic Goal #4
  • 288.05 is All Star Goal #5
  • 525.02 is World Cup Goal #2
  • 897.75 is Playoff Goal #75

In a few cases this cataloging produced duplicate floats which were resolved by incrementing the NHL regular season goal number by one.

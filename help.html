Assist Search

Assist data is stored in two formats:

[Primary Assist Name] [Secondary Assist Name]

  • Nicklas Backstrom TJ Oshie
  • Dylan Strome Pierre Luc Dubois
  • Kevin Shattenkirk

P:[Primary Assist Name]

S:[Secondary Assist Name]

  • P:Nicklas Backstrom
  • S:Pierre Luc Dubois

Date Search

Dates are stored in three formats:

[Month]/[Day]/[Year] (no leading zeros)

  • 1/13/2010
  • 11/5/2022

[Month Name] [Day] [Year] (no leading zeros)

  • December 10 2013
  • March 20 2025

and [Month Name] [Year]

  • November 2018
  • March 2024

Goal Search

Goals are identified by a unique number.

NHL regular season goals are indexed 1 to 929. All other goals are intertwined as floats with format

[NHL Regular Goal].[Other Goal]

  • 0.02 is World Championship Goal #2 (2005)
  • 21 is NHL Goal #21 (2005)
  • 36.04 is Olympic Goal #4 (2006)
  • 288.05 is All Star Goal #5 (2011)
  • 525.02 is World Cup Goal #2 (2016)
  • 897.75 is Playoff Goal #75 (2025)

In a few cases this cataloging produced duplicate floats which were resolved by incrementing the NHL regular season goal number by one.

URL Queries

Ovechkin App accepts URL queries to return specific goals (?1 to ?929) or a set of filtered search results (?fleury+golden-knights).
